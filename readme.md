# Waida Bridge

Waida Bridge is a proposed platform designed to help people discover government benefits, understand their financial options and connect with trusted organisations. The project will initially focus on Bristol, helping residents access support such as Pension Credit through benefits guidance, adviser referrals and connections to appropriate financial services.

The dataset selected is Pension Credit in Bristol by Ward, published by Bristol City Council. It contains ward-level information, including ward codes, ward names, sex, reporting periods and recorded Pension Credit recipient numbers. The dataset will help identify areas for community outreach and inform research into barriers to accessing financial support. As the data is historical, it will be supplemented with more recent information where available. It will be used for aggregate outreach planning, not individual eligibility or creditworthiness decisions.
